# deskpet

This is an internet radio and alarm clock that can function as a general internet connected hub. it's made up of two boards - one with a screen, buttons, and rotary encoders for controls, and the other with an ESP32-S3-WROOM, MAX98357A amp, speaker cable connectors, and an LED. Tne two boards are connected via a flat flex cable, and sit inside a 3D printed case. it is powered via a USB-C cable at the back. 

Given the ESP32-S3 can connect to the internet, in addition to functioning as an internet radio and alarm clock, the device will also be able to fetch RSS streams and check weather information.
